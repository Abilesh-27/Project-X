## Brand & Style

The design system embodies institutional stewardship, community solidarity, and modern administrative efficiency. It serves cooperative labour leaders, administrators, and union directors who require uncompromised clarity, rigorous record-keeping, and ethical transparency.

The visual style is **Corporate / Modern** anchored in civic stability and structural authority:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, community-oriented, reliable, and deliberate.
- **Atmosphere:** An uncluttered, high-accountability workspace that elevates manual labour governance into a precise, modern digital standard.
- **Visual Tenets:** High contrast between data metrics and backgrounds, crisp architectural boundaries, purposeful badges, and functional restraint.

## Colors

The palette balances administrative authority with organic, community-grounded growth and selective operational alerts:

- **Primary (#1E3A8A - Indigo Navy):** The structural core. Used for sidebar navigation, primary actions, table master controls, and institutional headers. Conveys governance and legal integrity.
- **Secondary (#15803D - Forest Green):** Represents labour dignity, operational health, active statuses, and cooperative dividend growth. Applied to success states, verified credentials, and affirmative actions.
- **Tertiary (#D97706 - Amber Gold):** Dedicated to attention-driven operational elements: ratings, certifications nearing expiration, pending approvals, and high-priority metrics.
- **Neutral (#0F172A - Deep Slate):** Anchors typographic hierarchy, ensuring maximum contrast and legibility across complex tables and reports.
- **Surfaces & Tones:** Background surfaces rely on clean white (`#FFFFFF`) with backing canvases in slate mist (`#F8FAFC`) and structural dividers in muted gray-blue (`#E2E8F0`). Dark surfaces (`#0F172A` and `#1E293B`) are strictly reserved for the global command sidebar and system navigation modals.

## Typography

The typography system relies on a unified implementation of Inter to achieve mechanical precision and tabular alignment:

- **Metrics & Numbers:** All administrative tables, member counts, and currency metrics must use font feature settings `cv02`, `cv03`, `cv04`, and tabular figures (`tnum`) to ensure aligned vertical scanning.
- **Hierarchy Structure:** Display and Headline roles structure high-level dashboard summaries. Title roles define card headers, section titles, and modal headers. Body sizes support deep data density without fatigue. Label roles are strictly applied to micro-badges, uppercase category kickers, and table column headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8px base rhythm that expands or condenses depending on data density requirements:

- **Desktop (1280px+):** Fixed 260px administrative navigation sidebar alongside a 12-column fluid content area. Gutters are fixed at 24px (`space-24`), and canvas margins are 32px (`space-32`). Multi-column card arrangements reflow into 2, 3, or 4 equal partitions.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Sidebar collapses into an icon-only rail (72px). The main layout transitions to an 8-column grid with 16px (`space-16`) gutters and 24px (`space-24`) margins.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Navigation transitions to a drawer panel. The grid becomes 4-column fluid with 16px margins. High-density data tables transform into stacked attribute cards to avoid horizontal clipping of critical member data.
- **Vertical Density:** Dense data listings utilize compact 8px padding increments; executive overview surfaces and ledger cards leverage 16px and 24px internal paddings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses low-contrast borders combined with soft ambient shadows to maintain a reliable, paper-like institutional feel:

- **Surface Baseline (`elevation-0`):** Canvas background `#F8FAFC`. Used for global backdrop and inactive track lanes.
- **Cards & Data Panels (`elevation-1`):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a subtle outline (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient shadow (`box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`).
- **Interactive Hover & Dropdowns (`elevation-2`):** Elevated cards on hover, popovers, and select menus use (`box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`).
- **Modals & Overlays (`elevation-3`):** Member inspection sheets, legal attestations, and audit confirmations use (`box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`) over a deep slate scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.6)`).

## Shapes

The design system maintains a structured, composed shape profile:

- **Soft Form Factors:** Standard inputs, buttons, and status chips apply a disciplined 0.25rem (`4px`) to 0.375rem (`6px`) corner radius.
- **Cards & Panels:** Primary content cards and table wrappers use `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px) to establish clear structural separation without appearing juvenile or overly playful.
- **Dialogs & Large Containers:** Administrative modal dialogues, drawer sheets, and major metric containers use `rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px).
- **Avatars & Member Icons:** Retain a subtle rounded square profile (`rounded-md`, 6px) to reinforce an official badge/credential appearance, avoiding circular avatars except for quick user profile drop-offs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1E3A8A`, text `#FFFFFF`, subtle border inset, 0.375rem radius. Hover shifts to `#1E293B`.
- **Secondary / Cooperative Action:** Background `#15803D`, text `#FFFFFF`. Used for positive operational actions (e.g., "Disburse Fund", "Verify Member"). Hover shifts to `#14532D`.
- **Outline / Tertiary:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Hover background `#F1F5F9`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#EF4444`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover background `#DC2626`.

### Badges & Status Chips
Status chips follow a consistent semantic formula featuring a tinted background, matched border, and deep contrast text:
- **Verified / Valid / Completed:** Background `#DCFCE7`, border `1px solid #86EFAC`, text `#14532D`.
- **Expiring / Pending Review:** Background `#FEF3C7`, border `1px solid #FCD34D`, text `#78350F`.
- **In Progress / Active Shift:** Background `#DBEAFE`, border `1px solid #93C5FD`, text `#1E3A8A`.
- **Suspended / Revoked:** Background `#FEE2E2`, border `1px solid #FCA5A5`, text `#991B1B`.

### Data Tables & Lists
- Table headers utilize uppercase `label-md` with neutral slate fill `#F8FAFC` and subtle bottom border `#E2E8F0`.
- Alternating or clean bordered rows with a baseline row height of 48px to support efficient desktop scanning.
- Interactive rows transition to `#F1F5F9` on hover.

### Inputs & Controls
- **Text Fields:** Height 40px, surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, 0.375rem radius. Focus state displays a crisp outline ring (`2px solid #1E3A8A` with a 2px offset).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Solid fill `#1E3A8A` when active with high-contrast white glyphs. Neutral border `#94A3B8` when unselected.

### Metric Cards & Ledger Panels
- Encased in `rounded-lg` containers with 1px border `#E2E8F0` and `#FFFFFF` background.
- Employs split headers: metric title in `body-sm` slate text, main value in bold tabular numbers, and auxiliary status badge positioned top-right.
